Roshni Ramesh — Retail experience designer

TATA.ev

Move with meaning

TATA Motors, which has been going strong on EV technology and focusing on democratizing access to electric mobility, wanted to create a store experience that would present a compelling consumer-facing brand entity epitomizing the essence of the electric revolution.

The challenge

Customers know switching to EV is the right, responsible move, but they don’t know where to begin, what questions to ask, and whose advice to take.

Client
TATA Motors
Space
EV experience store
Scope
Experience strategy, zoning, customer journey, interiors, fixtures and materials

The ripple effect

Our zoning principle was inspired by the concept of a ripple effect, beginning with your entry into the community and culminating in becoming an integral part of this movement through collaboration.

  1. You

    Welcome to your first step into the world full of meaningful opportunities

  2. Connect

    Meet a larger community who share the same aspiration of living purposefully

  3. Inspire

    Seeking knowledge through immersive engagements

  4. Evolve

    Showcases your future with EV and the larger impact your choices can make

The journey

Our journey was mapped keeping in mind our core idea of ‘move with meaning’, making the spatial interactions community-driven, purposeful and engaging.

01Arrive

A tranquil green sanctuary that clears the mind and welcomes customers with a sense of calm, gently guiding them into a space filled with unexpected opportunities.

02Connect

Breaking the cycle of sales-forward automotive experience, the space shifts perceptions of what the brand is about by starting the conversation over a cup of coffee at the full-service café, inviting customers to start a conversation.

03Inspire

Embarking on a transformative journey into the EV lifestyle through immersive experiences that inspire and deepen understanding of products and communities, cultivating a strong commitment to sustainable mobility.

04Evolve

Nurturing collaborations with both brands and people committed to eco-consciousness, we can cultivate a vibrant community dedicated to environmental sustainability, where shared values and actions contribute to a healthier planet.

The details

  • Interactive walls

    Inviting visitors to embark on a journey of discovery and intrigue, our display systems feature interactive, scannable units that unveil additional information and enhance learning experiences.

  • Smart systems

    Our slat wall displays are endlessly reconfigurable, and can accommodate varied posters, objects, and merchandise to keep the space looking new and fresh without having to re-construct the displays.

  • Recycled materials

    We designed furniture and wall finishes from recycled plastic panels, repurposing locally sourced waste into new products and visibly supporting the circular economy.

  • Local collaborations

    We partnered with local brands to source materials for our lights, furniture, rugs, and biophilia. This approach minimized travel and energy costs, while supporting and promoting local businesses and creators.

The impact

  • A strong, sustainable community

    TATA.ev’s refreshed strategy and design have boosted its impact and community engagement across platforms. The store now hosts workshops, from breakfast drives to beach clean-ups, yoga, sustainable cooking and recycled crafts, positioning it as a hub for sustainable living.

  • A new automotive experience

    Redefining the traditional automotive experience from a transactional buying experience to a community-driven movement that encourages conversations, learning and participation, making the store a space that brings people together for a better future.

  • Architectural transformation

    The site was entirely transformed with the addition of a mezzanine floor, which accentuates the height and enhances the architectural impact of the structure, all while staying true to the original design concept and experience.
