Snapshots

Three more brand spaces: a studio for fashion and craft, a coffee-first café and a flagship tyre experience centre.

  • USHA

USHA

Studio of Fashion and Craft

The challenge

The team at USHA approached us to develop and design a studio space where users could experience heightened learning, creativity and community engagement by exploring sewing as a hobby.

The framework

A space embodying the essence of a ‘creative playground’: a playful framework that offers infinite possibilities for co-working and learning, designed to encourage creative exploration and make the experience your own.

Dunkin’

The theatre of coffee

The challenge

Dunkin’ has a very strong donut-forward legacy; we needed to reposition it as a coffee-first brand. In the world of ‘me-too’ coffee brands, Dunkin’ needs to be distinct; however, as a challenger brand it has to play to the ‘right coffee’ category codes.

The concept

Celebrating Dunkin’s brand legacy with confidence and pride through a bold, vibrant approach that celebrates coffee. Creating a world that is filled with energy and positivity to make every coffee your own.

A warm, welcoming approach that is all about the celebration of coffee the Dunkin’ way and helps cultivate connections. An inviting space infused with pops of colour, along with a fun, positive brand voice.

Bridgestone

Experience centre

The challenge

We were tasked by Bridgestone Corporation to create a new flagship signature store for Bridgestone in India. The challenge was to transform a mundane service engagement into a knowledge-enhancing and interactive experience.

The concept

We created a category-breaking omnichannel vision that sets new levels of experience for selling tyres. We celebrated driving and reaffirmed Bridgestone’s leadership qualities.

We empowered and educated customers through interactive physical and digital touchpoints. This helped them understand and navigate the product range with ease, leading to an informed decision.
