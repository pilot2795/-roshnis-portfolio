# Roshni's portfolio

Portfolio website of Roshni Ramesh, retail experience designer.

**Live site:** https://pilot2795.github.io/roshnis-portfolio/

## Pages

- `index.html`: home, about and contact
- `tata-ev.html`, `tata-cliq-palette.html`, `zee5-loft.html`: case studies
- `snapshots.html`: USHA, Dunkin' and Bridgestone

Styles are in `css/styles.css`, scripts in `js/main.js` and images in `assets/img/`. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step: open `index.html` in a browser to preview it. Every push to `main` updates the live site.

## Before you share it

Placeholder text is highlighted in yellow and wrapped in `<span class="ph">`. Replace the text and remove the span.

- Your email, LinkedIn, phone and city appear in the footer of every page. Update the `mailto:` and LinkedIn links too.
- On the home page, fill in your city, years of experience, work history and education.
- On each case study, fill in your role, location and year.
- Add your CV as `assets/roshni-ramesh-cv.pdf`, then delete the "Add your CV" note next to the Download CV link.
