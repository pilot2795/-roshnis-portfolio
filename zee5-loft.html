ZEE5 Loft

Loft of fun — creating living portals to digital worlds

ZEE5, a global digital streaming platform and entertainment destination, was looking to expand its audience base. They approached us to create an experience to attract the millennial demographic.

The approach

We conceptualized and designed 8 regionally localized cafés to help ZEE5 proactively reach its millennial audience.

Client
ZEE5
Space
Entertainment cafés
Scope
Café concept, zoning, interiors and multi-city roll-out
Locations
Bengaluru first, then Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur and Chandigarh

Design explorations

We explored a spectrum of possibilities to expand our thinking and creative outcome. One route was playful, interactive and conversational while the other was relaxed, inspired and witty.

Development

We detailed out our design with a clear experience that defined each zone. The driving thought was the idea of making the space interactive and building touchpoints that engage customers, making them want to come back for more.

  1. Zone 1

    • Double height
    • Community
    • Beverage counters

  2. Zone 2

    • Transition
    • Open kitchen
    • Library

  3. Zone 3

    • Group seating
    • Amphitheatre
    • Entertainment

The layout we created was based on mood zones developed to match a customer’s ideal journey in the space. Zone 1 is a high-activity zone with opportunities to interact. Zone 2 is a seamless transition space with pops of interest along the way. Zone 3 is a space to unwind, game and enjoy a moment of relaxation.

The experience

Facade

A striking, dynamic fascia that showcases the brand graphics and personality. A clear view into the café and its bustling activities, enticing passers-by.

Map wall

An interactive map wall highlighting the key locations of Bangalore greets you as you enter. Customers are encouraged to contribute to the wall by filling in their personal experiences of Bangalore.

Open kitchen

The open kitchen becomes the heart of the café, featuring food at its core. This can be experienced throughout the café, along with interactive elements like a DIY smoothie cycle.

Cosy nooks

Creating cosy nooks for people to work and hang out in groups, with booth seats that feature a chalkboard wall for collaboration. This area also transforms into a stage for events and performances.

Library

A transition space that allows customers to discover an extensive library of books, board games and video games.

With ZEE5 Loft, we have entirely re-imagined what it means to be a café. From just a place to eat and meet, to a theme park of joyous activities and food theatre.

Roll-out

The overwhelming success of the first café gave ZEE5 the confidence to try this concept out in other cities across Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur and Chandigarh. Each of these was designed as an ‘urban chameleon’ of localized character, reflecting its own city.
