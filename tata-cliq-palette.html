Tata CLiQ Palette

A beautiful obsession — a personalized journey of self-expression

Tata CLiQ set out with a grand ambition: to revolutionize beauty by bringing personalization to the forefront. Our mission was clear: to establish a brand that embodies this transformative vision.

The challenge

In today’s beauty landscape, customization is often lacking. Finding products that truly suit your unique needs can be a challenge, as is finding a trusted source for recommendations. Navigating through cluttered stores and selecting from uncurated assortments only adds to the frustration. It’s a cycle of sticking to familiar routines and looks, rather than exploring new possibilities.

Client
Tata CLiQ
Space
Beauty store
Scope
Brand space concept, zoning, customer journey, fixtures and lighting

A space where obsession comes alive

The space, enveloped in just two contrasting colours, white and red, represents purity and passion – two key attributes of beauty. The obsessive red amidst a sea of textural whites creates peaks within the consumer journey to make it uniquely memorable.

The journey

  1. Arrival

    A visually striking storefront with an interactive digital screen, which becomes a dynamic reflection of the brand’s voice that builds curiosity.

  2. Beauty playground

    Escape into a parallel phygital universe of limitless beauty and personalized discoveries, and meet a like-minded, beauty-obsessed community.

  3. Category browsing

    Curated browsing and need-based selection, sprinkled with deeper interactive product knowledge and trial experiences that accelerate decision making.

  4. Check-out

    Self-service and assisted transactions layered with personalized gifting to cater to all demographic needs of the beauty enthusiasts.

  5. Leaving

    An ownable ritual that instills strong brand credibility and recall value, and makes the customer feel special and included.

Get set, play

At the core is the pulsating community space titled ‘The Beauty Playground’ — a hub for product discovery, with a custom ceiling and a handcut tiled circular floor in our signature obsessive red shade. Surrounding this centre piece, concentric looping lights, both at the ceiling and floor, immediately draw the eye to our hero experience zone.

Bespoke masterclass and magic mirror tables inspired by the soft textural yet bold brand language, double up as intimate or community settings by merely drawing the bold red curtains that truly make your time at the playground feel special.

Learn a new smokey eye with your BFFs or sink into a stool as you test out a hot new lip shade on the custom Magic Mirrors experience table for endless virtual make-up try-ons.

Beauty is in the details

The fixtures are designed with thoughtful details to enable ever-changing and playful product interactions. The curved open forms enable intuitive exploration.

Adjustable trays & movable components build a modular user-centricity.

Utilities like mirrors and vanity kits are carefully integrated within fixtures for efficient browsing.

Fixtures crafted from layers of pristine white PU, soft acrylics & a textured Corian bring premiumness to the palette. While the muted colours allow the products to shine in all their glory, the pops of red details highlight curations, product trends and stories.

Discovering beauty rituals

The fluid, curved wallbays mimic the contours of our Scoop language and are rooted in the graceful movements of beauty rituals.

Gondolas

The mid-floor Gondolas are designed as robust yet soft brand capsules that reduce clutter in a noisy multi-brand environment.

Lighting

The lighting design takes you on a continuous journey of beauty obsession. In the landscape of a minimal material palette, the light is crafted as an iconic key component of the store aesthetic. Crafted as individual pieces put together and suspended near the open ceiling, it dances along and accentuates the contours of the fixtures.
