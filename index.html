About

I’m Roshni, a retail experience designer with 10+ years of experience creating brand spaces.

I design physical brand experiences from the first idea to the last detail: the concept, the zoning and customer journey, and the fixtures, materials and lighting that bring a brand to life. My work spans automotive, beauty, entertainment, food & beverage and lifestyle — from single flagships to concepts rolled out across cities.
