About

I’m Roshni, a Design Director at Landor with 10+ years designing brand spaces across retail, hospitality, experience centres and learning institutes.

I lead projects from research and strategy through concept, design development and execution. My work spans automotive, beauty, entertainment, food & beverage and lifestyle, from single flagships to concepts rolled out across cities.

Alongside client work, I lead and mentor Landor’s experience design team and was selected for its Future Leaders Collective.
In 2025 I co-hosted the Building Immersive Brand Worlds workshop at Kyoorius Design Yatra.
